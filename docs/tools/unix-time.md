# Unix Time

Convert Unix timestamps to human-readable dates and vice versa.

## How to use

1. Enter a Unix timestamp (seconds or milliseconds) or pick a date/time.
2. View conversions in local time and UTC.

## Example

| Timestamp | Date (UTC) |
|-----------|------------|
| `1700000000` | Nov 14, 2023 |

## Tips

- Paste a 10-digit (seconds) or 13-digit (milliseconds) number anywhere to auto-open this tool.
- Shows relative time (e.g. "3 days ago") when applicable.
